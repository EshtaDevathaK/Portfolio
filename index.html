Section 03

Design Process

A structured five-stage methodology that transforms user needs and business goals into scalable interfaces.

01

Understand

Discovering user goals, market research, business constraints, and defining core problem statements.

02

Structure

Mapping user flows, content hierarchy, information architecture, and core interaction patterns.

03

Wireframe

Creating low and mid-fidelity wireframes to iterate swiftly on layout composition and usability.

04

Design

Crafting pixel-perfect high-fidelity visual interfaces with consistent typography, color palettes, and design tokens.

05

Prototype & Handoff

Building interactive prototypes, conducting user testing, and delivering production-ready developer specifications.
